# Hi, I'm Eman Bilal!

I'm an economics, finance, and quantitative research professional with an interest in applied economics, public policy, statistical modelling, and data-driven decision-making.

I hold a BS (Honours) in Economics from Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS) and a Master in Management from HEC Paris, specializing in Strategic Management.

My academic research has involved econometrics, computational macroeconomics, statistical modelling, and social network analysis. Professionally, I worked in institutional equity research at Société Générale and AllianceBernstein, where I was a named co-author of more than 60 research publications.

- Computational Economic Modelling
- Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics

## Research Projects

I'm currently organizing and documenting several of my academic research projects, including:
